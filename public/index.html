    Plug the chatpad into the controller and connect the controller to the PC. Press every key. Dashed keys (+, -, X1, X2, green, orange, mic, the two icon keys) are not learned yet: click the key on screen, then press it on the chatpad. If nothing happens, click "No event": that key is handled by Windows or the chatpad and never reaches the browser. Green or orange + a letter types a symbol: it shows under Alt codes.

    Rollover and ghosting

    Hold keys down one at a time and watch "Max held at once". If a held key does not light up, the keyboard dropped it (ghosting or rollover limit). Try common groups: W A S D + Shift + Space, and Q W E R T.
